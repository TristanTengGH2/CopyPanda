# CopyPanda mobile companion idea

Saved November 6, 2026. Status: possible future direction for review; not approved scope or a commitment to build.

A mobile app could carry the same personal panda from the desktop to a library or an in-person study session. Its main value would be continuity, easy body doubling, and studying with the phone put away.

## Possible additions

1. **Pick up where you left off.** Share the saved task, next step, leaves, and study calendar between desktop and phone. Opt-in accounts and reliable cross-device sync would come before a separate mobile release. Keep solo guest use available. Email sign-in is currently pending.
2. **Join friends easily.** Scan a QR code, enter a short code, or open an invite link. Show friends' pandas beside yours and offer optional synchronized timers. Build on the existing room system without requiring location tracking or video calls.
3. **Study with the screen off.** Let someone start a session, lock their phone, and read a physical book, with a notification at the end. Decide and test mobile background behavior explicitly; phone locking should not automatically inherit desktop lock-to-pause behavior.
4. **Give leaves a small, satisfying use.** Begin with a few selectable sweaters, mugs, or desk decorations. Let people choose a saving goal before expanding the shop.
5. **Offer a useful weekly recap.** Summarize study days, time spent on subjects, and the unfinished bookmark. Encourage returning after missed days without resetting all progress.

## Possible first mobile experience

Use three main destinations: **Study, Together, and Progress**. Put the panda and timer first, with notes, customization, and settings accessible within those screens.

Keep the basic loop short: choose or resume a task, start or join a session, study, then optionally save a reflection or next step.

## Suggested sequence to consider

- Establish opt-in cross-device accounts and sync, including consistent reward balances.
- Add a small customization selection that gives earned leaves a purpose.
- Test a compact mobile website with a handful of people before committing to native mobile development.
- Use what people return for to define a native app, with platform-specific background timer and notification testing.

The proposed distinction is a personal companion that follows the user between devices and shared study sessions, with gentle accountability. Timers, rewards, and group focus already exist in products such as [Forest](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/forest-focus-for-productivity/id866450515).

## Questions for later

- Which mobile platform should come first?
- Is the main use solo screen-off studying, joining friends, or continuing desktop work?
- What should happen to an active session when changing devices or losing connectivity?
- What background behavior and notifications can the chosen mobile implementation reliably support?
- Which first few cosmetic items would make earned leaves feel worthwhile?
